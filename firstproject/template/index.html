The Roots of
Bagicha

Born from a desire to return to the soil, Bageecha is more than a resort; it is a living, breathing ecosystem. Nestled amidst 50 acres of organic orchards, our sanctuary offers a silence that is heard and a peace that is felt.

Every stone used in our architecture tells a story, and every meal served is a blessing from our own harvest. Experience the luxury of simplicity.
