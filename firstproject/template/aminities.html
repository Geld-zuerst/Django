Gastronomy

The Taste of Earth

Farm-to-Table Dining

Our kitchen has no secrets, only fresh ingredients. 80% of what is on your plate comes directly from our 50-acre organic farm. Pick your own vegetables in the morning, and our chefs will transform them into a royal feast by dinner.

  • 100% Organic Produce
  • Traditional Wood-Fired Ovens
  • Private Orchard Dining
Signature Experience

The Barn Brewery

Hidden inside a reclaimed barn, our micro-brewery crafts small-batch artisanal beers using local wheat and fruits from our orchard (Mango Ale is a favorite). A rustic setting to unwind after a day in nature.

  • Craft Mango & Guava Ales
  • Live Acoustic Evenings
  • Bar Snacks Menu
Rejuvenation

Wellness & Spa

Veda Spa

Authentic Ayurvedic treatments using oils made from herbs grown in our own garden. Experience the Abhyanga massage for deep relaxation.

Yoga Shala

An open-air pavilion facing the sunrise. Join our resident yogi for complimentary morning sessions or book a private meditation hour.

Infinity Pool

A temperature-controlled saltwater pool that creates an illusion of merging with the lake below. Includes a separate kids' zone.
