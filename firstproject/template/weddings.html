Frequently Asked Questions

What is the guest capacity?
We can accommodate up to 600 guests in our Orchard Lawn for the main event, and we have 45 rooms available for resident guests.
Is outside catering allowed?
To maintain our quality standards and farm-to-table philosophy, all catering is provided in-house by our expert chefs.
Do you provide decor services?
Yes, we have an in-house decor team specializing in sustainable and rustic themes. However, we also allow empanelled outside decorators.
